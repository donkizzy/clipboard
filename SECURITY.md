# Reporting a security or privacy issue

Email the maintainer privately at [hello@amosgodwin.com](mailto:hello@amosgodwin.com) with the subject “Clipboard security report”. Include the Clipboard version, macOS version, relevant capture or link-preview settings, and what you expected to happen.

Use a synthetic clipboard item or URL to illustrate the issue. Keep passwords, access tokens, private URLs and history databases out of reports and public issues. The maintainer can follow up by email for any further details.
